Allowlist

Applied at promotion, not at read — adding an entry unlists the address on the next pass; deleting one does not re-block anything until it is reported again. A prefix shorter than /24 (IPv4) or /48 (IPv6) is refused unless force is checked — 0.0.0.0/0 here would silently disable the entire feed.
